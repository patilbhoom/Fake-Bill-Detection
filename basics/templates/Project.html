Prediction on Fake Bills

Using Machine Learning and Django

Predicting the future of fake bills suggests that while technology to create highly realistic counterfeits may improve, advancements in security features on genuine currency, like color-shifting ink, intricate microprinting, and sophisticated watermarks, will likely make it increasingly difficult for counterfeiters to produce convincing fake bills, leading to a potential decrease in the prevalence of high-quality counterfeit currency; however, vigilance and awareness among individuals and businesses remain crucial for identifying potential fakes.

Description of the Dataset

  • is_genuine -> (Target Variable)

    True (1)=Genuine Bill
    False (0)= Fake Bill

  • diagonal → Length of the bill's diagonal (in mm)

    A counterfeit bill may have slight variations in its diagonal measurement.

  • height_left & height_right → Heights of the left & right sides of the bill

    Fake bills may have uneven height proportions compared to real ones.

  • margin_low & margin_up → Margins at the top and bottom of the bill

    Counterfeit notes often have misaligned margins due to printing errors.

  • length → Overall length of the bill

    Fake bills might deviate slightly from the original dimensions.
